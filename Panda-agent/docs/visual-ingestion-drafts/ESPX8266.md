## Reviewer must check

1. Print case: front/back use lower-case "3v3" in boxes; confirm the stored form should keep it as printed.
2. Whether S0/"SO" is the letter O or a zero: the photo shows "SO" next to "SC" and "SK"; transcribed as printed letters, not verified.
3. The numbers "LED - 2 / RGB - 12 / B1 - 13" are printed text only; the photo gives no meaning.
4. Reconcile silkscreen "B1" and "Flash" with stored "User programmable" and "FLASH".
5. The shield "MODEL" and "VENDOR" lines show no values in the photo.
6. 15 pads per row were counted from labels; the listing's "~11 usable GPIOs" is a different claim and must not be merged with it.
